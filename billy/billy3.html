Early Life

Billy Herrington was born in Long Island, New York, and worked in the fitness industry before becoming a model and actor. He quickly gained attention for his charming looks and strong personality.

Viral Moments

Billy's popularity skyrocketed when clips from his videos were uploaded to NicoNico Douga. The edits and memes that followed led to the creation of the famous “Gachimuchi” videos, bringing Billy unexpected fame.
